# EventApp user stories and role features

**Status:** Canonical story and scope catalogue for team review. These 31 stable story IDs and their role-feature tables are the source of truth for story wording and scope. [Project context](../workflow/sdd/project.md) owns the phase-priority roadmap and accepted RD01-RD07; detailed acceptance criteria currently exist for [F01](../workflow/sdd/features/F01-published-programme.md). A catalogue row is not implementation approval.

EventApp has three product workspaces. Role columns show who supplies or consumes a capability, never which teammate implements it. A dash means no direct user-facing feature for that role. Delivery order is maintained once, in the project roadmap; this catalogue groups stories for review.

Each story is intended to become a scoped capability specification. Retain its ID and link it to detailed observable behaviour, exclusions, contracts, numbered acceptance criteria, applicable security rules, test scenarios and revision-specific evidence. Existing F01 acceptance IDs remain stable; shared contracts and cross-story integration checks must remain linked when specifications become smaller. Draft stories and schema fields do not approve implementation.

### Shared foundation

F10 identity verifies sign-in; server permissions restrict attendees to their private records, organisers to assigned events, and business members to their business across its event workspaces. Changing screens grants no authority. Business member roles/invitations and organiser provisioning remain open decisions.

F10 bounded AI jobs persist owner, progress and failure state, enforce time/usage/tool limits, cancel between steps, minimise outbound data and validate results before display. Jobs may create authorised drafts but cannot approve applications, publish announcements or execute external actions autonomously. Build identity/permissions with the first F01 slice, business checks with F06, and job controls before role AI features. Exact-action approval precedes any external send or execution.

## Readiness and shared constraints

- Refine stories into approved specifications before implementation; use the [agent workflow](../workflow/agents/README.md) for independent test design/review, protected baselines, actual handoffs and verification.
- Initial application review is proposed for business applications only; speaker submissions require a separate submitter design and scope decision. Confirm rubric fields and applicant-visible reasons.
- Confirm hard constraints, travel/break buffers, unknown-accessibility handling, identity/provisioning, hosting, SoC LLM data policy and numerical usage limits before the affected capability depends on them.
- Multiple booths or activities may run in the same room at overlapping times for all product roles; room sharing alone does not block publication or browsing. An attendee adding an activity that overlaps one already in their itinerary sees a warning regardless of whether the rooms match. Manual drafts may retain overlaps; accepting an itinerary still requires the existing RD03 hard constraints to pass.
- Capacity is a declared per-item limit, not available inventory or a reservation guarantee. Do not infer aggregate room occupancy from overlapping booth activities without a separately approved model.
- All three workspaces retain core manual workflows when AI fails or another role is offline. AI cannot silently replace accepted records.
- Use authorised data, validated schemas and factual references; test injection, privacy, stale output, limits, failures and approval bypasses under [security rules](../workflow/sdd/security.md). Research requires sources/timestamps/uncertainty; screened links do not guarantee supplier safety.
- Module boundaries, relationship constraints, missing booth/setup and participation-projection contracts, migrations and integration checkpoints remain in the [schema plan](../workflow/sdd/schema-and-parallel-plan.md). Accepted plans remain domain records rather than generic AI artifacts. Meal/route entries, wishlists and optional image/export designs require explicit refinement.
