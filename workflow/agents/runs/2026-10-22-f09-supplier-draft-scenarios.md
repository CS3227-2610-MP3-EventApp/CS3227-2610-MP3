## Product/architecture choices to return to human owner

1. Research discovery: selectable curated public domains, user-entered supplier URLs, or search-provider discovery; exact provider/key/budget/terms and permitted fetch domains. A simple LLM supplier list without real evidence does not satisfy real sourced research.
2. Network boundary: HTTPS/443 vs additional ports; URL canonicalisation; public-address rules; DNS/peer pinning/TLS, redirects/proxies, content/bytes/decompression/time limits. Security defaults can be proposed but cannot silently promise SSRF prevention without a complete design.
3. Pricing: SGD-only vs FX; quantities/packs/MOQs/tier math; tax/shipping unknowns; what is extracted supplier fact vs user/model estimate; maximum quantities/prices and total semantics.
4. Saved-state lifecycle: creator-private vs shared-business scope; eligible participations; immutable evidence vs mutable shortlist; exact CAS/dedup; refresh/staleness threshold; retention/delete/archive and Phase3 snapshot dependency.
5. Partial-success/AI grounding: minimum evidence, strict claim provenance, manual editing limits and labels, automatic-vs-explicit saving; clearly state no semantic truth/seller safety/current stock warranty.
6. Shared job extension: exact search/fetch/model call ceilings/timeouts/retries; shared quotas and environment gating; disclosure/selected outbound facts and provider retention unknowns.
