## Product outcome, users and scope

A business researches printers, snacks and freebie suppliers using live, attributable supplier evidence and compares observed listing prices and delivery constraints at a requested quantity. Results form a saved private shortlist, not a safety guarantee, current-inventory guarantee, quotation, order or purchase. Model-generated text without a retrievable supplier source is not research evidence.

## Use cases and user interface

An authorized business owner selects a participation, material category, quantity, delivery area/date and optional constraints, then reviews an asynchronous research result. Show each source's supplier, source URL, provider, retrieval time, captured price basis/currency, quantity assumptions, delivery evidence, unknowns and explicit uncertainty. Let the requester save a shortlist, reopen it and refresh it as a new comparison without losing the prior saved comparison. Show partial results and allow manual work to continue when search fails. Links are external and never carry a trust guarantee.

## Inputs, outputs and functional contract

### Approved v1 input and output contract

Each request is bound to an existing participation currently owned by the caller's active business membership. Any extant participation qualifies regardless of Submission state. POST `/api/v1/business/participations/{participationId}/supplier-research` accepts only `{requestId:UUID,category:printing|snacks|freebies,brief:string,quantity:integer,deliveryArea:string,neededBy?:ISO-date,dataSharingConfirmed:true}`; unknown fields are rejected. `brief` is 1-1000 trimmed Unicode code points; quantity is 1-1,000,000; deliveryArea is 1-100 coarse Singapore region code points and excludes street/contact details. No event/setup facts or revisions are sent. Explicit disclosure precedes each request; neither brief nor deliveryArea is claimed scrubbed or automatically free of personal data.
POST returns 202 with the `BusinessSupplierResearchJob v1` body directly. GET on the same participation route accepts optional query `limit` and `offset` and returns `{items:SupplierResearchSnapshotV1[],total:integer,limit:integer,offset:integer,latestJob:BusinessSupplierResearchJobV1|null}` newest-first. `limit` defaults20 and is 1-100; `offset` defaults0 and is a non-negative integer. Unknown query fields are rejected. Snapshot detail/delete is GET/DELETE `/api/v1/business/supplier-research/{snapshotId}`; DELETE requires query `expectedRevision=1`, returns204, or returns409 `in_use` if that snapshot is selected in the shortlist. Clear the selection using shortlist CAS before retrying. Shortlist GET/PUT is `/api/v1/business/participations/{participationId}/supplier-shortlist`; PUT fully replaces `{expectedRevision,selectedOffers:[{snapshotId,offerId}]}`, rejects unknown fields/foreign offers, duplicate `(snapshotId,offerId)` pairs, and more than100 selections, and returns the new shortlist revision. Missing shortlist is `{id:null,participationId,revision:1,createdAt:null,updatedAt:null,selectedOffers:[]}`; persisted shortlists have UUID `id` and ISO-instant timestamps. First PUT creates revision2. Use PUT with an empty list to clear selections. Current owner, participation and creator-private checks precede resource details; mutations enforce trusted Origin and all responses are no-store. Errors: 401 `unauthenticated`, 403 `forbidden`, non-disclosing 404 `not_found`, 409 `request_conflict|active_job|revision_conflict|in_use`, 422 `validation_error`, 429 `quota_exceeded`, 503 `service_unavailable`.

The additive `supplier_research` workflow is defined by [shared AI task contract revision 6](../shared/monitor-and-recover-ai-tasks.md). It reuses common business controls: requester-scoped request-ID deduplication, 10 jobs/user/UTC day, one active business job, 60-second provider timeout, 180-second total deadline, 40,000 outbound code points, 4,000 output tokens, 40,000 response-content characters, at most two provider requests per worker, shared 30/minute provider gate, one retry only after explicit HTTP 502/503, and 30-day job/artifact retention. One Tavily Basic Search API request ([endpoint](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-reference/endpoint/search), [credits](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-credits)) uses `auto_parameters:false`, `topic:general`, `country:singapore`, `include_answer:false`, `include_raw_content:false`, `max_results:3`; fetch up to three result pages and make one configured SoCLaaS extraction call. No autonomous model/tool loop. At most three candidate pages are independently fetched. Redirects and retries are additional HTTP attempts; all retrieval attempts including Tavily search, page fetches, redirects and retries share a cap of five. Tavily credentials are backend-only. The shared contract specifies job/result/error DTOs; this amendment does not change existing workflows.

Research uses Tavily for live candidate discovery and server-side retrieval of up to three candidate pages; only fetched page content can ground a saved offer. Tavily snippets are discovery metadata, not price/delivery evidence. Search receives only disclosed category, quantity, brief and coarse delivery area/date. Page GET receives only a server-selected URL and ordinary headers; no user query/brief, cookies, auth/session headers, referrer, contacts or account identifiers. Treat all search/page/model text as hostile; instructions cannot grant tools or alter policy. Model may extract but may not invent supplier identity, URL, value, retrieval time or evidence. Retain field-level source URL, final redirected URL, provider, time and bounded excerpt/location for seller/product/price/currency/quantity/pack/MOQ/shipping/tax/delivery. A numeric match, excerpt or schema-valid result does not prove semantic association; ambiguous product/variant/pack/currency/qualifier remains unknown. Never treat cart totals, empty-cart zero, unrelated prices or expired quote terms as listing prices. A useful offer requires a fetched public page grounding supplier/product identity. Revision 4 (D06): an offer whose supplier name and product description are each a literal case-insensitive substring (at least 2 characters) of a verified quote, both quotes from the **same** fetched page, is accepted as a **listing-only offer** even when no price basis or delivery constraint is source-supported. Every unsupported comparison field (price, delivery, minimum order, availability; an unsupported subtotal is covered by the price reason when price is also unknown) is null with a **server-written** fixed-wording unknownFields reason (model-written reasons for those fields are discarded, and unknownFields entries naming fields outside the allowed field set are dropped); it never carries an unsupported value and is never labelled a quotation. Offers with a supported price or delivery are unchanged and need the existing exact-context validation. An offer is still rejected when its quote is absent from the cited page, the source ID is forged, identity is not literally supported, a supplier/product name is under 2 characters or contains a currency amount, URL, email or phone-like digit run, or the output is malformed; any such per-offer defect (including a fabricated quote) fails the whole extraction as before. One useful offer (comparison-backed or listing-only) yields a saved complete/partial snapshot; zero useful offers fails without a snapshot. Offers rejected only for unsupported price/delivery evidence are retained as listing-only offers rather than omitted. Status is `partial` when any source failed or was omitted, or when any offer is listing-only. Failures/omissions are visible. Cancellation, revocation or policy failure before commit prevents any late save. Refresh creates a new immutable snapshot.

`SupplierResearchSnapshot v1`: `{id:UUID,participationId:UUID,revision:1,createdAt:ISO-instant,status:"complete"|"partial",request:{category,brief,quantity,deliveryArea,neededBy},offers:SupplierOfferV1[1..3],sources:[{sourceId:UUID,searchResultUrl:string,requestedUrl:string,finalUrl:string,provider:"tavily_page_fetch",retrievedAt:ISO-instant,isStale:boolean,excerpt:string}],sourceFailures:[{sourceId:UUID,code:"unsafe_destination"|"fetch_failed"|"unsupported_content"|"too_large",displayMessage:string}],isStale:boolean}`. `isStale` is true when any evidence source is older than 24 hours. Offer v1 is `{id:UUID,supplierName:string,productDescription:string,evidenceByField:[{field:string,sourceId:UUID,evidenceQuote:string,sourceLocator:string|null}],listedPrice:{amountMinorUnits:canonical-nonnegative-integer-string-0-to-18-digits|null,currency:string|null,unitLabel:string|null,quantityBasis:positive-integer-string-1-to-7-digits|null},estimatedItemSubtotalMinorUnits:string|null,estimatedCurrency:"SGD"|null,deliveryEstimate:string|null,minimumOrderQuantity:integer|null,availability:string|null,unknownFields:[{field:string,reason:string}],qualifications:string[]}`. Evidence source records are server-owned as listed in `sources`; raw extraction output is a strict object `{offers:[{supplierName,productDescription,evidenceByField:[{field,sourceId,evidenceQuote,sourceLocator}],listedPrice:{amountMinorUnits,currency,unitLabel,quantityBasis},deliveryEstimate,minimumOrderQuantity,availability,unknownFields:[{field,reason}],qualifications}]}` with 1-3 offers and no additional fields. `SupplierOfferField` is exactly one of `supplierName|productDescription|listedPrice.amountMinorUnits|listedPrice.currency|listedPrice.unitLabel|listedPrice.quantityBasis|deliveryEstimate|minimumOrderQuantity|availability|qualifications`. Each evidence quote is 1-1000 Unicode code points. Each offer has at most10 qualifications, each 1-500 code points. Minor-unit amounts are canonical decimal strings `0` or `[1-9][0-9]{0,17}`. Quantity basis is a positive canonical decimal string of 1-7 digits and cannot exceed1,000,000. Shortlists contain at most100 unique `(snapshotId,offerId)` references; duplicates are rejected. Each evidenceByField requires a server-issued `sourceId`; there is no offer-level `sourceId`. The persisted server-derived offer adds `id`, `estimatedItemSubtotalMinorUnits`, and `estimatedCurrency`. `quantityBasis` is a positive integer string meaning count of identical listed units per listed price; a derived subtotal is allowed only for exact integer multiples. Tax and shipping are unknown unless independently evidenced; evidenced terms appear only in source-backed `qualifications` with matching `evidenceByField`, and are always excluded from item subtotal. The server maps source IDs to canonical URLs/times and verifies quote presence; URL/time provenance is never model-authored. Quote matching supports traceability but does not prove semantic truth. Excerpts are bounded plain text and untrusted. `SupplierShortlist v1` is `{id:UUID,participationId:UUID,revision:integer,createdAt:ISO-instant,updatedAt:ISO-instant,selectedOffers:[{snapshotId:UUID,offerId:UUID}]}`. One creator-owned shortlist exists per participation; PUT replaces selected IDs under `expectedRevision` CAS. Missing shortlist is `{id:null,participationId,revision:1,createdAt:null,updatedAt:null,selectedOffers:[]}`; persisted shortlists have UUID and ISO timestamps; first write creates revision2. Snapshot content is immutable. Totals are SGD only; non-SGD listings may be shown as source evidence but are not converted or totaled. Use exact integer minor units; calculate subtotal only when quantity/pack/tier basis exactly supports requested integer quantity, with no rounding or extrapolation. Subtotal excludes unsourced shipping/tax/fees. Ambiguous price associations remain unknown. Refresh creates a new snapshot and preserves shortlist references unless explicitly changed.

### Approved source and network policy

Approved source mode is broad Tavily search followed by independently checked direct page retrieval; curated-only mode is not selected. Search returns at most three candidates. Application never fetches caller-supplied URLs or follows arbitrary links found in page text. Candidate URLs come from Tavily then pass server policy. Public HTTPS redirects may go to a different public host, at most two hops, revalidated at each hop. Record final URL; this does not verify seller identity.

Page-fetch policy: HTTPS port 443; reject userinfo, executable schemes, IP literals, unsafe DNS (reject if any A/AAAA answer is non-global), and ambiguous canonical forms. Pin checked public IP per connection while retaining original hostname for TLS SNI/certificate validation; disable environment proxies, cookies and auth. Revalidate each redirect, max two hops. Permit `text/html`/`text/plain` only; 10-second timeout plus overall remaining deadline, and 1 MiB wire and decoded body per page; Reject non-identity content encoding; unsupported compression fails that candidate. At most three candidate pages are independently fetched. Redirects and retries are additional HTTP attempts and count toward the shared maximum of five total retrieval attempts including Tavily search; redirects do not consume an additional candidate-page slot. Unsafe URL, DNS/peer policy failure, bad content type/size fails that candidate; if transport cannot enforce policy globally, fail the whole job without a snapshot. Other valid candidates may still produce partial success. Evidence links have no automatic preview/fetch and imply no seller trust, identity or suitability.

Approved persistence: creator-private comparisons and shortlist, accessible only while the creator has current business authority and owns the participation. Saved snapshots and shortlist remain until creator deletion; jobs/transient artifacts expire after 30 days. Evidence becomes stale 24 hours after retrieval. Explicit refresh creates a new snapshot, never modifying prior evidence or selection. Revocation denies access; no provider-side deletion claim is made.

This scope does not send quotation requests, contact suppliers, publish shortlist data, place orders, or spend money. Future quotation/procurement is covered by separate capability contracts and approval boundaries.
